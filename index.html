The problem: nobody can see inside a used battery

The battery is the most expensive part of an electric vehicle, yet its condition is a guess.

🤷

Buyers guess

A used pack looks the same with 80% or 40% of its life left, so honest sellers get lowballed.

🏦

Lenders can’t price risk

Financing an electric vehicle has no battery-health data behind it, so loans cost more or never happen.

♻️

Good packs get scrapped

Healthy batteries are thrown away because no one can prove they still work.

How PackPulse works

Three steps from a sealed battery to a trusted price.

🔌

1. Clip on

Attach the module to the battery. It measures voltage, current and temperature on every charge and ride.

📱

2. Read the score

The app turns raw readings into a health score, remaining range and a resale grade.

🔗

3. Share the report

Send a verified battery report to a buyer, repair shop or lender with one link.

Questions

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Does the score replace a lab test?

No. It’s a practical estimate from real usage data, accurate enough to price and compare batteries. Lab-grade testing is a separate, much more expensive process.

Which batteries does it work with?

The first version targets common lithium-ion and LiFePO4 packs from 12 V to 72 V, the kind used in e-scooters, e-bikes and electric rickshaws.

Can it damage my battery?

It’s designed as a passive monitor that draws a very small load and never controls charging or discharging.

When can I buy one?

PackPulse is in the prototype stage. Join the pilot below to be among the first to test it.

What does it cost?

Pricing is not final. Riders will pay for the module and a small yearly fee, while fleets pay monthly per battery.
